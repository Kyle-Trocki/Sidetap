# Sidetap acceptance audit

Reviewed October 1, 2026. Actions run on a double tap or a triple tap anywhere on the desk. Four broad zones remain for calibration and the zone accuracy test: rear and front on the left of the MacBook, and rear and front on the right. The zone accuracy test is balanced at 15 held-out double taps per zone, 60 total.

Deterministic regressions reject a plosive-plus-voiced speech candidate and elevated background while preserving short and resonant taps. Talking can also be captured as a profile-specific negative. The active probe is filtered out of onset detection. In live use, taps within 300 ms of a physical key press or click are ignored, and no actions run above a room noise limit. On one setup, the typing check removed all 38 detections in 45 seconds of typing, and 2 minutes of loud-room audio produced no gestures. | Automated verified; measured on recordings from one setup |

## Remaining completion gates

The MVP must not be called physically validated until all of the following are captured on the target MacBook and desk:

1. A passive/active/hybrid comparison using real taps.
2. A fresh four-zone calibration in the final laptop position.
3. A balanced zone accuracy report with at least 80% accuracy and median response below 200 ms.
4. Double and triple taps confirmed to run their actions in live use, including the Paste text and screenshot actions.
5. A 30-minute live microphone/action run including taps, conversation, typing, laptop touches, and representative background noise.
6. A visual interaction review of every screen at normal and minimum window sizes.

On one MacBook Pro and desk, gates 2 and 3 are met. Gates 1, 4, 5, and 6 are still open.
